AICompany Policies and Guidelines
Last Updated: November 2024

Section 1: Work Schedule and Attendance

1.1 Working Hours
AICompany operates on a flexible work schedule. Core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, during which all team members should be available for meetings and collaboration. Employees may choose their start time between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, completing an 8-hour workday.

1.2 Remote Work Policy
AICompany supports a hybrid work model. Employees may work remotely up to 4 days per week, with at least one day in the office for team collaboration (typically Wednesdays). Fully remote positions are available for certain roles with manager approval.

1.3 Attendance and Punctuality
Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance and notify their team lead at least 2 hours in advance if they will be late or absent. Excessive tardiness (more than 3 instances per month) may result in a performance review.

1.4 Time Tracking
All employees must log their working hours daily using the company's time tracking system (Jira/Tempo). Time entries should be submitted by end of day Friday for the current week.

Section 2: Leave and Time Off

2.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
- New employees: 18 working days per year
- After 2 years: 20 working days per year
- After 5 years: 24 working days per year
PTO accrues monthly and can be used for vacation, personal matters, or sick leave.

2.2 Sick Leave
Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible when taking sick leave. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate may be required.

2.3 Public Holidays
AICompany observes all official public holidays in the country of employment. The holiday calendar is published at the beginning of each year.

2.4 Parental Leave
- Maternity leave: 126 calendar days (as per local legislation)
- Paternity leave: 14 calendar days
- Adoption leave: Available upon request, discussed case-by-case

2.5 Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave may be granted for personal reasons with manager and HR approval. Requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

Section 3: Compensation and Benefits

3.1 Salary Payment
Salaries are paid twice monthly: an advance on the 20th and final payment on the 5th of the following month. Payments are made via bank transfer to the employee's designated account.

3.2 Annual Review and Raises
Performance reviews are conducted annually in December. Salary adjustments are based on performance, market conditions, and company results, typically effective from January 1st.

3.3 Bonuses
- Performance bonus: Up to 15% of annual salary based on individual and company performance
- Referral bonus: $500-$1500 for successful employee referrals (paid after 3 months of employment)
- Project completion bonuses: Determined on a project basis

3.4 Health Insurance
AICompany provides comprehensive health insurance coverage for all full-time employees after a 3-month probation period. Coverage includes medical, dental, and vision care.

3.5 Professional Development
- Annual learning budget: $1000 per employee for courses, conferences, and certifications
- English language courses: Fully covered by the company
- Technical certifications: Reimbursed upon successful completion

3.6 Equipment and Home Office Setup
- Company laptop provided to all employees
- Home office allowance: $300 one-time setup budget
- Monthly internet reimbursement: Up to $30

Section 4: Code of Conduct

4.1 Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct, treat colleagues with respect, and contribute to a positive work environment. Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated.

4.2 Dress Code
Business casual attire is expected in the office. When meeting with clients, business formal attire is required. Remote workers should maintain professional appearance during video calls.

4.3 Communication Standards
- Respond to emails within 24 hours during business days
- Be available on Slack during core working hours
- Use professional language in all business communications
- Keep your calendar updated with availability and time off

4.4 Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company information, client data, or proprietary code to unauthorized parties. This obligation continues even after employment ends.

Section 5: Technology and Security

5.1 Equipment Usage
Company equipment should be used primarily for work purposes. Personal use is permitted within reason but should not interfere with work responsibilities.

5.2 Data Security
- Use strong passwords (minimum 12 characters with mixed case, numbers, and symbols)
- Enable two-factor authentication on all company accounts
- Never share passwords or access credentials
- Lock your computer when away from desk
- Report any security incidents immediately to IT

5.3 Software and Tools
Only approved software may be installed on company devices. Requests for new software should be submitted to IT department for security review.

5.4 BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)
Personal devices accessing company resources must comply with security policies including device encryption and remote wipe capability.

Section 6: Client Interaction

6.1 Client Communication
All client communication should be professional, timely, and accurate. Copy your project manager on important client emails. Never make commitments without consulting your team lead.

6.2 Client Meetings
- Arrive 5 minutes early for all client meetings
- Come prepared with agenda and necessary materials
- Follow up with meeting notes within 24 hours
- Dress professionally for video calls

6.3 Confidentiality
Client information is strictly confidential. Do not discuss client projects in public spaces or with unauthorized personnel.

Section 7: Project Management

7.1 Agile Methodology
AICompany follows Agile/Scrum methodology for most projects. Team members are expected to participate in:
- Daily stand-ups (15 minutes)
- Sprint planning (beginning of each 2-week sprint)
- Sprint retrospectives (end of each sprint)
- Backlog grooming sessions

7.2 Task Management
All tasks must be tracked in Jira. Update task status daily and log time spent. Blockers should be reported immediately in stand-ups or to the project manager.

7.3 Code Quality
- All code must be peer-reviewed before merging
- Follow company coding standards and style guides
- Write unit tests for new features (minimum 80% coverage)
- Document complex logic and APIs
- Use meaningful commit messages

7.4 Deadlines
Meeting deadlines is critical. If you anticipate missing a deadline, notify your team lead at least 48 hours in advance with a revised estimate.

Section 8: Career Development

8.1 Career Paths
AICompany offers clear career progression paths for technical and management tracks. Discuss your career goals with your manager during quarterly one-on-ones.

8.2 Mentorship Program
New employees are assigned a mentor for their first 3 months. Senior employees are encouraged to participate as mentors.

8.3 Internal Mobility
Employees are encouraged to explore opportunities within the company. Internal job postings are shared in the #careers Slack channel.

8.4 Performance Improvement Plans
If performance issues arise, a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) may be implemented. PIPs typically last 30-90 days with clear goals and regular check-ins.

Section 9: Office Environment

9.1 Office Hours
Office is open Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Access cards are required for entry.

9.2 Kitchen and Break Areas
- Clean up after yourself
- Label personal food items
- Coffee and tea are provided free of charge
- Lunch break is 1 hour (not included in 8-hour workday)

9.3 Meeting Rooms
Book meeting rooms through the office calendar system. Meetings should start and end on time. Clean whiteboards after use.

9.4 Noise Levels
Respect quiet zones. Use meeting rooms or phone booths for calls. Keep conversations at reasonable volume in open office areas.

Section 10: Termination and Offboarding

10.1 Notice Period
- Employees: 2 weeks notice required
- Company: 2 weeks notice (or payment in lieu)
- Senior positions: 1 month notice from both parties

10.2 Exit Interview
All departing employees are invited to participate in an exit interview with HR to provide feedback.

10.3 Return of Company Property
All company equipment, access cards, and materials must be returned on or before the last working day.

10.4 Final Payment
Final salary payment includes prorated salary, unused PTO payout, and any outstanding bonuses, paid on the next regular pay date.

Section 11: Compliance and Legal

11.1 Equal Opportunity
AICompany is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, gender, age, national origin, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

11.2 Anti-Harassment Policy
Harassment of any kind is prohibited. Report any incidents to HR immediately. All complaints are investigated promptly and confidentially.

11.3 Whistleblower Protection
Employees who report violations of company policy or illegal activities in good faith are protected from retaliation.

11.4 Compliance Training
All employees must complete annual compliance training covering data protection, security, and workplace conduct.

Section 12: COVID-19 and Health Policies

12.1 Illness Policy
Stay home if you feel unwell. Do not come to the office with symptoms of contagious illness.

12.2 Vaccination
While not mandatory, vaccination is strongly encouraged. Paid time off is provided for vaccination appointments.

12.3 Office Safety
Follow all posted safety guidelines. Hand sanitizer and masks are available throughout the office.
